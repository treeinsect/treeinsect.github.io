The Ballmerlyser
A functioning breathalyser which detects when your blood alcohol level meets the Ballmer Peak, using an alcohol sensor, a microcontroller, and various taped-together breadboards. Made for HackNotts 2024.


Melody Mapper
A robot which will draw a picture based on whatever song you play it. Constructed as a series of Python scripts, made for HackSheffield 2024

Five Nights in A32
A FNAF 1 remake made in Pygame, set in the University of Nottingham's Jubilee Campus. Made for Royal Hackaway 2024.

Logic Poker
Made in Godot with character models made in Blender for HackSussex 2025, Logic Poker is an Inscryption-inspired card game intended to teach formal reasoning/Lean 3.
