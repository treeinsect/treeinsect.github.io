About Me

I'm Will, a second year Computer Science student at the University of Nottingham.

I made this website to show off everything I'm involved in - whether that be hackathons, personal projects, or extracurriculars.

I'm General Secretary of HackSoc at Nottingham, as well as President of Cheese Society. I also recently became an official organiser of HackNotts!

I have attended 4 hackathons at time of writing, and hope to attend many more. You can see what I made at each by clicking the portfolio tab.

As well as liking computers, I'm also an artist! I mostly do digital illustration, but I recently started learning to crochet. You can also see my creative projects in my portfolio.
