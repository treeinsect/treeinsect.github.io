Welcome to the website of Will Wright!

This page is currently under construction, so for the time being, here are some of my past projects, WIPs, and things I'm involved in!

WIPs

Point and Click Personal Website - Get to know me through a raw HTML5 mystery game, complete with graphics modelled and textured by me.

Culkulator - A short form which calculates what percentage of Rory Culkin's filmography you've seen. I'm at 80%!

Past Projects

The Ballmerlyser - A functioning breathalyser which detects when your blood alcohol level meets the Ballmer Peak, using an alcohol sensor, a microcontroller, and various taped-together breadboards. Made for HackNotts 2024.

Melody Mapper - A robot which will draw a picture based on whatever song you play it. Constructed as a series of Python scripts, made for HackSheffield 2024

Five Nights in A32 - A FNAF 1 remake made in Pygame, set in the University of Nottingham's Jubilee Campus. Made for Royal Hackaway 2024.

Side Stuff

Mentor Scheme - I volunteered for and was chosen as a 2nd Year Mentor on the University of Nottingham Computer Science course. This involves running/participating in course events, answering questions from the year below, and providing a point of contact for a group of 15 mentees. It's a lot of fun!

Brothers Cream Productions - An unofficial and unincorporated production company, working on making silly films for fun. Currently in preproduction on Lenton Chainsaw Massacre.
